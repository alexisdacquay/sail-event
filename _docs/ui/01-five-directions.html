Five ways to lay out Sail-event

Same data every time: one saved search, a run in progress, three events found tonight, the calendar feed. Each direction decides what the page is about. Pick one, or point at parts of two.

What it is for People who think in days. The question "what's on tonight" is answered by looking, not reading.

What it is for One question, one answer list. The thresholds become a visible line on the ladder instead of a settings slider.

Strong Radically simple; the number explains itself; nothing to learn.

Risk Dates and places are secondary to the score, so a user scanning for "Saturday" works harder; several searches need a switcher.
